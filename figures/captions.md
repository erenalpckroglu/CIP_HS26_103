# Figure captions

### rq1_rent_by_rooms.png
Gross monthly rent (CHF) by number of rooms (rounded to 0.5) for 13,987 listings. Boxes show the interquartile range with the median as line, whiskers extend to 1.5 x IQR, outliers of the boxes are not drawn. Groups with fewer than 20 listings are omitted; n per group is given below each label. Medians are in tables/rq1_rent_by_rooms.csv.

### rq1_spearman.png
Spearman rank correlation between the gross monthly rent (CHF) and six property characteristics of Swiss rental apartments (Flatfox, November 2026). Each bar uses all listings with both values present (n = 9,964 to 14,065); all p-values are listed in tables/rq1_spearman.csv. Colour separates numeric variables from yes/no features.

### rq2_price_by_distance.png
Price per m² (CHF per month) of 11,683 Swiss rental apartments with known living area, grouped by straight-line distance to the nearest railway station (Flatfox, November 2026; stations: atlas service points of the SBB). Boxes show the interquartile range with the median as line, whiskers extend to 1.5 x IQR, outliers of the boxes are not drawn; n per band is given below each label. Medians and quartiles are in tables/rq2_bands.csv.

### rq3_income_scatter.png
Mean taxable income per taxpayer (ESTV, tax year 2022, CHF per year) against the median advertised rent per m² (CHF per m² per month) for 485 Swiss municipalities with at least 5 listings that have a known living area (Flatfox, November 2026). The area of each point is proportional to the number of listings; the cities of Zürich and Luzern are labelled. Spearman correlations are in tables/rq3_spearman.csv.

### rq3_map_price_m2.png
Median advertised rent per m² (CHF per m² per month) by municipality, in five quantile classes. Thin grey lines are municipality borders (swissBOUNDARIES3D 2026), dark lines canton borders. Light grey municipalities have insufficient data (fewer than 5 listings with known living area): 1620 of 2110. Municipalities on Lake Geneva include their share of the lake in the official boundary data (wedge shapes); the other lakes are not part of any municipality.

### rq3_maps_panel.png
Spatial pattern of the municipality characteristics used for RQ3: a) median advertised rent per m² (CHF per m² per month, quantile classes), b) mean taxable income per taxpayer (CHF per year, 2022, quantile classes), c) population density (inhabitants per km², logarithmic colour scale), d) number of Flatfox listings. Panels a to c show only municipalities with at least 5 listings that have a known living area; the others are light grey. Dark lines are canton borders. Municipalities merged after 2022 have no income value.
